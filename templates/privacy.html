Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account, along with the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to help keep the Service secure and reliable.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide core features of Spendly, including logging expenses, generating spending summaries, and personalizing your experience. We may also use aggregated, non-identifying data to improve the Service. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting and infrastructure. These providers only process data as necessary to deliver their services to us and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided within the app, or via your account support channel.
